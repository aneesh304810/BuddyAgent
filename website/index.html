Why Buddy is different

Most apps do one job and forget you between visits. Buddy does all the jobs, knows you, and keeps getting better.

One app instead of twenty

Email, calendar, reminders, to-dos, budgets, subscriptions, card due dates, travel deals, research, notes and translation, in one conversation.

A memory that knows you

Buddy remembers your facts, documents, people and preferences, finds them by meaning, not just keywords, and tidies its memory every night.

Grows with what you need

Ask for something it can't do yet. Buddy designs the new ability, safety-checks it and switches it on only after your yes.

Learns your taste

From what you tell it and the choices you make, Buddy learns your style for places, travel and money, so its picks fit you.

Does the work, you decide

It drafts, compares, fills in and prepares. Sending, buying, booking and paying always wait for your yes, checked in code.

Private by design

Your data lives on a private, encrypted server. It's never sold, never used for ads and never used to train AI.

What we believe

Your data stays yours

We never sell data, show ads or use your data to train AI models.

You stay in charge

Our AI asks before it sends, buys, books or changes anything. Your yes is checked in code, not left to the AI.

Read-only money access

Bank connections are read-only. Buddy can't move money, make payments or change your accounts.

Least data, shortest time

We collect only what a feature needs, and delete it automatically when it's no longer useful.
