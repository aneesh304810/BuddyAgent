Buddy, your personal assistant

Talk to Buddy from your phone or headset. It knows your day, keeps an eye on your inbox and your money, and asks you before it does anything that matters.

What Buddy does

Money at a glance

Spending by category, budgets, subscriptions and price rises, card due dates, minimums and limits, with a weekly report and a monthly check-in.

Inbox and calendar

Important emails flagged, bills and receipts found, rules you approve, and reminders before events.

Travel deals

Compares flights and hotels from several sources, points out hidden fees and fine print, and links you to book yourself.

Voice first

Hands-free on your phone and headset, with clear cues when Buddy has heard you.

Connecting your bank with Plaid

Buddy uses Plaid to connect to banks and card accounts, read-only.

  • You sign in to your bank inside Plaid's own window. Buddy never sees or stores your bank password.
  • You choose which accounts to share, bank by bank.
  • Buddy reads transactions, balances and card details (due dates, minimums, limits, APR). It can't move money or make payments.
  • Unlink any bank at any time in Buddy or at my.plaid.com. Access is revoked and that bank's data is deleted.
