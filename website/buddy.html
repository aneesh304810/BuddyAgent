Buddy, your one personal assistant

Talk to Buddy from your phone or headset. It connects your apps, remembers your life, watches your money, finds the best deals, and learns new skills when you ask.

What Buddy does

Talk, hands-free

Natural conversation that keeps listening between turns. Interrupt any time. It learns your voice, so only you count in Talk mode, and it gives clear cues when it heard you.

Memory and documents

Share documents, photos, PDFs and spreadsheets. Buddy files them, pulls out the important facts, people and dates, and answers from them later. Sensitive documents stay in a vault it asks before opening.

Money

Spending by category, budgets with warnings, subscriptions and price rises, card due dates, minimums, limits and APR, which card to use for rewards, goals, a weekly report and a monthly check-in.

Inbox and calendar

Every email account sorted by priority, urgent mail flagged right away, bills and receipts found, rules and label suggestions you approve, reminders before events, and a morning briefing.

Travel and deals

Flights and hotels compared across several sources on the total price, hidden fees and fine print. It watches routes and prices, learns what fares are normal, and you book on the provider's site.

Places and rides

Restaurants, hotels and activities picked for your taste, checked against reviews and Reddit. Uber or Lyft opens with your drop-off already filled in.

Research and reports

Background research that reads real sources and reports back with a spoken summary. It can also make charts, diagrams, Word documents and slide decks.

Languages

Listen-in gives you the gist when people around you speak a language you don't know. An English coach also gives gentle tips on how you speak and write.

Organiser and forms

To-do lists, goals and check-ins by voice. Buddy can draft answers to a form in your browser; you check them and press Submit yourself.

Your other apps

It connects thousands of everyday apps through standard connectors. Lookups just happen; changes ask first.

How Buddy improves itself

Buddy gets better in two ways: it learns you, and it grows new abilities on request.

1. You ask

"Buddy, can you track my package deliveries?" If it can't do that yet, it says so and offers to build it.

2. It builds

Buddy researches the service, writes a small new ability and tests it in an isolated sandbox.

3. It checks

Automatic safety checks plus a second AI's review look for anything unsafe before you see it.

4. You approve

Nothing runs until you say yes. Changes to Buddy's own code go to the developer for review. Buddy never releases changes to itself on its own.

  • Learns your preferences from your choices, like Uber or Lyft and which flights you open, and marks what it learned so you can correct it.
  • Tidies its memory every night: merges duplicates, flags facts that disagree, and finds dates to remind you about.
  • Knows its own costs and picks the most economical AI model that does each job well.

Connecting your bank with Plaid

Buddy uses Plaid to connect to banks and card accounts, read-only.

  • You sign in to your bank inside Plaid's own window. Buddy never sees or stores your bank password.
  • You choose which accounts to share, bank by bank.
  • Buddy reads transactions, balances and card details (due dates, minimums, limits, APR). It can't move money or make payments.
  • Unlink any bank at any time in Buddy or at my.plaid.com. Access is revoked and that bank's data is deleted.
