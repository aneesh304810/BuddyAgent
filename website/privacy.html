Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

This policy covers Buddy, made by Buddy Labs. It explains what data Buddy handles, why, how it's protected, who processes it and how long it's kept. Buddy is currently in private beta.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your data, or share it for marketing or advertising.
  • We never use your data to train AI models or to make credit decisions.
  • We never collect your bank password, and Buddy can't move money or make payments.

Consent

You consent to each source before any data is collected: in Plaid Link for each bank, through Android permission screens, and by adding email or calendar accounts yourself. You can withdraw consent at any time by unlinking the account or turning off the permission.

How we use data

Only to give you answers and reminders about your own life and money. That includes spending by category, budgets, subscriptions and price rises, bills and card due dates, answers to your questions, calendar help and travel checks.

How we protect it

Data is stored encrypted on a private server that isn't reachable from the public internet, with multi-factor authentication and role-based access. See Security for details.

Your choices and rights

  • Unlink a bank in Buddy or at my.plaid.com.
  • Turn off any phone permission in Android settings.
  • Ask Buddy to forget a document, a preference or your voiceprint.
  • Ask us to access, correct, export or delete all your data: email aneeshnair304810@gmail.com. We act within 30 days.

Children

Buddy isn't for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact
