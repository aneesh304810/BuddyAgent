# Buddy by Buddy Labs: brand strategy and guidelines

Status: **designed** (assets generated; not yet in the app or on the website).

## Strategy

**Promise:** Your intelligence. Your life. One place.

**Positioning:** a personal AI assistant you talk to first, that quietly brings together your day, your money,
your health, what you're researching and what you're buying, and never acts without you.

**Personality:** premium, minimal, intelligent, trustworthy, personal, approachable, transparent, privacy-first.
In practice: calm surfaces, one green accent, plain words, every judgement with its reason.

**What Buddy is not:** a generic chatbot, a bank app, a wall of dashboards, an AI admin console.

**The three ideas every screen carries:** the conversation is the product; the intelligence works behind the
experience; the user stays in control.

**Chosen:** concept 3's wordmark with a mark taken from its own "b": the same stroke, with a short tail at the foot
of the stem so the letter also reads as a speech bubble. One idea runs through the name, the icon and the product
(the conversation is the product). It holds up at 16 px (checked in the contact sheet).

## Using the logo

- **Clear space:** at least the height of the mark's bowl (the "o" of the b, 40 units of the 96 grid) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** lockup 96 px (or 20 mm) wide; mark 16 px, using `small-mark-24.svg` below 32 px.
- **Colour:** mark in primary green, wordmark in text ink; on dark, light green and light ink. One colour where
  colour isn't possible.
- **Don't:** stretch, outline, add shadows or gradients, rotate, put the mark in a speech bubble (it already is one),
  set the wordmark in a font, or place it on busy photos without a solid panel.
- **Name:** "Buddy" in running text; "Buddy by Buddy Labs" where the company must be named. Product parts keep their
  names: Buddy Maestro, Buddy Connect, Privacy Gateway, Buddy Intelligence.
- **Public website:** pages never name vendors (the owner's rule).

so they can be told apart without reading the label (the label is always there too).

## Type

Inter on the web, Roboto (the system face) on Android; medium for headings, regular for text, tabular numbers
wherever digits line up. Scale (size/line height): display 32/40, title 24/32, 20/28, 17/24, body 16/24, small
14/20, label 14/20 medium, caption 12/16, overline 11/16 semibold. Tokens: `../06-design-system/typography.json`.

## Voice of the words

Plain, short and specific: "Logged 128/82 at 7:40 am", not "Your reading has been successfully recorded". Say where
a number came from. Say what Buddy will do before it does it. British spelling as today (fibre, colour). No
exclamation marks, no emoji in the interface.
